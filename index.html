01 / Why EprintsRepo?

EPrints, reinvented for secure, scalable access.

Go beyond repository access. Take control of your repository.

Standard EPrints provides the essential foundation for scholarly content. EprintsRepo takes it further with a governance layer for institutions that need precise control over who can see content, what they can manage, and how every important action is accounted for.

EPrints at the core. EprintsRepo in control.

02 / Access & governance

Control at the level that matters.

Every user, role, permission, and important action can be controlled and accounted for.

01

Granular access control

Create user groups and define what each group can access. Permissions can reach individual articles and items, keeping restricted content with the right people.

02

Three levels of authority

Viewers access permitted content. Administrators manage content and users. Super Administrators create and oversee administrators through a clear chain of responsibility.

03

Complete audit history

A dedicated web dashboard records important user and administrative activity, giving you a centralized answer to who did what, and when.

04

Security without complexity

Manage authentication, groups, roles, and permissions from one system instead of piecing together fragmented access controls.

05

EPrints at the core

Keep the proven repository foundation while adding the authorization, administration, and accountability your institution needs.

06
